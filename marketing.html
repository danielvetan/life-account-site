Features

Everything your family will need.
All in one place.

You don't know what you don't know. Life Account does, and walks you through it in the order that matters.

01 · LIFE INTERVIEW

A plan built for your family, in two minutes.

Ten quick questions about marriage, children, military service, home, crypto and pets turn on exactly the items that apply to your household.

02 · READINESS

See how prepared your family is, at a glance.

One suggested task each week. Critical gaps, like no guardian named for your children, are called out first. Items you haven't reviewed in a year fade back to "needs review".

03 · GUIDANCE

Plain-English guidance for every item.

Why it matters, how your family would claim it, and the common pitfalls, like why a will doesn't override an outdated beneficiary form.

04 · THE HAND-OFF

A hand-off you can actually test.

Your vault key is split into three Family Key Cards. Any two open it, and a recovery drill proves your family can get in before they ever need to.

05 · SURVIVOR MODE

A calm plan for the people you leave behind.

No folders and no percentages. Just what to do next, one step at a time, with the numbers to call and the letters you left them.

Military & VA, built in

DD-214, SGLI, the Survivor Benefit Plan, DIC, burial benefits and DEERS, with the steps survivors need to take.

Encrypted on your device

Everything is sealed with AES-256-GCM before it's saved, with the key in the Keychain. No account, no servers.

Face ID, twice when it counts

Unlock with Face ID or your passcode, and confirm again before passcodes, seed phrases or recovery details are shown.

Letters that matter

Write to your spouse and children, including letters marked to open at a milestone, like an 18th birthday.

Typos caught for you NEW

Each Family Key Card code carries a checksum, so a mistyped character is caught instead of silently failing.

A tamper-evident log

Every unlock, change and recovery is recorded in a chain that breaks if anyone edits or deletes an entry.

Family sharing SOON

Owners edit together from their own iPhones. Everyone else can view now or only when needed, and every invite needs every owner's approval.

Private until needed SOON

Keep any item, and every letter, readable only by you until the Family Key Card step, even inside a shared vault.

Verified in person SOON

A 20-digit safety number and QR code prove a new phone really belongs to your family member, not an impostor.

The hand-off

A key your family can actually use.

Apple's Legacy Contact passes your iCloud files to your family, but not your saved passwords or Keychain. So on its own, it can't open an encrypted vault. Life Account closes that gap.

KEY A

Legacy Package

Rides along in iCloud Drive, released by Apple to your Legacy Contact.

KEY B

Family Key Card

A printed card for your spouse or primary beneficiary.

KEY C

Your attorney

For when Apple isn't involved at all.

For the people you leave behind

A gentle plan, not a pile of paperwork.

Grief is hard enough. When your family opens your vault, Life Account shows them only what to do next, and tells them it's fine to wait.

  • The first days. The funeral home, death certificates, the phone passcode, and the joint account to use right now.
  • Income and benefits. Life insurance, employer benefits, Social Security and VA claims, with the numbers to call.
  • Accounts and the estate. The will, the mortgage, cards and subscriptions. There's no rush.
  • Your letters. Waiting for them whenever they're ready.
How it works

From "I should really do this" to done.

Answer the Life Interview

Ten quick questions about your household. About two minutes, and your plan is tailored to you.

Ten minutes a week

Work through one suggested item at a time. Your readiness score climbs, and reminders keep it current.

Hand off, then test it

Set your Legacy Contact, print your Family Key Cards, and run the recovery drill once a year.

Privacy

Your family's information stays with your family.

No servers, no sign-in, no analytics. We never see your vault. It's stored encrypted on your device and leaves it only through the Legacy Package you choose to create.

Read the privacy policy →
  • Encrypted on device. AES-256-GCM, with the key in the Keychain behind Face ID or your passcode.
  • Zero-knowledge hand-off. The Legacy Package can't be opened with fewer than two Family Key Cards.
  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs that phone home.
  • You're in control. Delete the vault and its key from the device at any time.
