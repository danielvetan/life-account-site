Features

You don't know what you don't know. Life Account does.

No empty folders. Just the things your family would actually need, explained in plain English, in the order that matters.

A plan built for your family

A short Life Interview (marriage, children, service, home, crypto, pets) turns on exactly the items that apply to your household.

Guidance for every item

Why it matters, how your family would claim it, and the common pitfalls, like why a will doesn't override an outdated beneficiary form.

A readiness score that's honest

See how prepared your family is at a glance, with one suggested next task each week. Items you haven't reviewed in a year fade back to "needs review".

Critical gaps, flagged

No guardian named for minor children? Nobody knows your phone passcode? The things that would hurt most are called out first.

Military & VA, built in

DD-214, SGLI, the Survivor Benefit Plan, DIC, burial benefits and DEERS, with the steps survivors need to take.

Encrypted on your device

Everything is sealed with AES-256-GCM before it's saved, with the key in the Keychain. No account, no servers.

Face ID, twice when it counts

Unlock with Face ID or your passcode, and confirm again before passcodes, seed phrases or recovery details are shown.

Family Key Cards NEW

Your vault key, split three ways. Any two cards open it; one alone reveals nothing. A checksum catches typos on the printed code.

A recovery drill NEW

Prove your family can get in before they ever need to. The drill rebuilds the key from the cards and confirms it matches.

Survivor Mode

Your family doesn't see folders. They see a calm plan, one step at a time, with one-tap calls and nothing they have to do today.

Letters that matter

Write to your spouse and children, including letters marked to open at a milestone, like an 18th birthday.

A tamper-evident log

Every unlock, change and recovery is recorded in a chain that breaks if anyone edits or deletes an entry.

The hand-off

Two halves of one key.

Apple's Legacy Contact passes your iCloud files to your family, but not your saved passwords or Keychain. So on its own, it can't open an encrypted vault. Life Account closes that gap.

KEY ALegacy PackageRides along in iCloud Drive, released by Apple to your Legacy Contact.
KEY BFamily Key CardA printed card for your spouse or primary beneficiary.
KEY CAttorneyFor when Apple isn't involved at all.
  • Any two cards open the vault. Apple's release plus your spouse's card, or your spouse plus your attorney.
  • One card alone reveals nothing, not even to someone who breaks into your iCloud account.
  • Lose a card? Re-split. New cards are issued and the old ones stop working.

Think of it like a safe-deposit box that needs two keys. Your family holds one, and Apple or your attorney holds the other.

For the people you leave behind

A gentle plan, not a pile of paperwork.

Grief is hard enough. When your family opens your vault, Life Account shows them only what to do next, and tells them it's fine to wait.

  • The first days. The funeral home, death certificates, the phone passcode, and the joint account to use right now.
  • Securing income and benefits. Life insurance, employer benefits, Social Security and VA claims, with the numbers to call.
  • Settling accounts and the estate. The will, the mortgage, credit cards and subscriptions. There's no rush.
  • Your letters. Waiting for them whenever they're ready.

Every step has "Later" and "Not needed". No percentages, no red, and support for grief always one tap away.

Privacy

Your family's information stays with your family.

Life Account has no servers, no sign-in and no analytics. We never see your vault. It's stored encrypted on your device, and leaves it only through the Legacy Package you choose to create.

Read the privacy policy →
  • Encrypted on device. AES-256-GCM, with the key in the Keychain behind Face ID or your passcode.
  • Zero-knowledge hand-off. The Legacy Package can't be opened with fewer than two Family Key Cards.
  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs that phone home.
  • You're in control. Delete the vault and its key from the device at any time.
