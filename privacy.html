Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · applies to Life Account for iOS, version 1.0

The short version: Life Account does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device, encrypted. We can't see it, and neither can anyone else unless you give them your Family Key Cards.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your vault (checklist progress, account and policy details, contacts, notes and letters) is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is saved, and stored only in the app's private storage on that device.
  • The vault key is stored in your device's Keychain, protected by Face ID or your device passcode.
  • The Legacy Package, if you create one, is written to storage you control (On My iPhone, or your own iCloud Drive). It is encrypted and cannot be opened without two of your three Family Key Cards.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software. We have no servers that receive your information.

Sharing

We don't share data because we never receive it. You decide who receives your Family Key Cards and Legacy Package. Anyone holding two cards and the package can open your vault, so keep the cards somewhere safe.

Apple services

If you store the Legacy Package in iCloud Drive or use Apple's Legacy Contact feature, Apple's privacy policy governs those services. Apple's Legacy Contact does not release Keychain passwords, which is why Life Account uses Family Key Cards.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete vault from this device in the app, or delete the app. This removes the encrypted vault and its key from the device. Copies you exported, and Legacy Packages saved to other locations, must be deleted from those locations.

Children

Life Account is intended for adults planning for their families. It does not knowingly collect information from children, and it collects no information from anyone.

Not professional advice

Life Account helps you organize information. It does not provide legal, financial, tax or medical advice.

Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that sends data off your device (for example an optional emergency-access service), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the feature will be opt-in.
